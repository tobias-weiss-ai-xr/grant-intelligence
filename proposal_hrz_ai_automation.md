# Proposal: AI-Automation Support for HRZ
## Funding Application Strategy for the HRZ Leadership Team

---

## Executive Summary

**Objective:** Secure funding to implement **AI-driven automation** for the *Hochschulrechenzentrum (HRZ)* at Philipps-Universität Marburg, enhancing **IT service efficiency, research support, and operational resilience**.

**Target Grants:** Three high-potential funding sources aligned with HRZ's mission and the university's digital transformation strategy.

**Estimated Budget Range:** €100,000–€1,500,000 (depending on scope and grant type).

**Timeline:** Rolling applications (DFG FDM, BMBF DigiServ) or project-based (BMBF Digital/AI).

---

## 1. Problem Statement

The HRZ faces increasing demand for **IT services, research computing, and data management** while operating with limited staff resources. Manual processes in **user support, system monitoring, resource allocation, and data curation** lead to:

- **Bottlenecks** in service delivery (e.g., ticket response times, account provisioning).
- **Inefficiencies** in resource utilization (e.g., underused HPC clusters, storage mismanagement).
- **Rising costs** due to repetitive tasks (e.g., log analysis, backups, compliance reporting).
- **Missed opportunities** for proactive problem-solving (e.g., predictive maintenance, anomaly detection).

**AI automation** can address these challenges by:
- Automating **routine IT operations** (e.g., incident classification, user provisioning).
- Optimizing **resource allocation** (e.g., dynamic HPC scheduling, storage tiering).
- Enhancing **security and compliance** (e.g., threat detection, data audit trails).
- Improving **user experience** (e.g., AI chatbots, self-service portals).
